Think about it

  1. Keep acceleration the same and double the mass. What happens to the force the engine needs?
  2. The engine maxes out at 12,000 N. As mass goes up, what happens to the acceleration you can actually get?
  3. Which trial had the fastest time? Use F = m × a to explain why.
  4. Big Rig Rosa’s engine pushes with more force than Zippy Zoe’s. Why does Zoe usually beat her?
  5. Nitro adds the same 6,000 N to any car. Would it help a 600 kg car or a 3,000 kg car more? Show the math.
  6. In Hairpin Canyon, why did you have to brake before tight turns? Why does a heavy car need to start braking earlier?
